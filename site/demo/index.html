First minute

  1. Press ENV 3 for a new kit.
  2. Press ARP 9 for a new pattern.
  3. Press PLAY Space, and leave it running.
  4. Play the instruments on A W S E D.
  5. Press LFO 4 to mutate the rhythm.
  6. Press SCL 2 to play the selected voice chromatically.
  7. Hold SAVE 8 and press ENV 3: a new sound for one voice.

About this demo

This is the real firmware on an emulated FM-1 CPU, in fm1-emu by Joakim Eriksson (MIT), at commit {{emulator-commit}}. Timing, sound and load here are the emulator's, not a device's. Saved kits and patterns stay in this browser.

The file it runs is for the emulator. It is not a release, and nothing here installs it on hardware: see installation status.
